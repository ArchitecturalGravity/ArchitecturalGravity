# Intent: Diagnose a plant problem by separating observed evidence from competing causes and preserving unresolved uncertainty

[Plant]:
- Crop: Tomato
- Identifier: T-4
- Growth stage: Early fruiting
- Bed: South vegetable bed

[ObservedEvidence]:
- Leaf symptoms:
  - Lower leaves yellowing
  - Several brown lesions with concentric rings
  - Upper new growth mostly unaffected
- Distribution:
  - Affected plants: 2 of 6 tomatoes
  - Neighboring basil: Unaffected
- Root zone:
  - Soil moisture at 5 cm: Damp
  - Standing water: Not observed
- Recent conditions:
  - Rainfall pattern: Four wet days during the previous week
  - Irrigation change: None reported
- Treatment history:
  - Fertilizer change: None reported
  - Pesticide application: None reported

[H1]:
- Hypothesis: Fungal leaf disease
- Expected evidence:
  - Lesion pattern consistent across older leaves
  - Symptom increase after extended leaf wetness
- Contradicting evidence:
  - New growth may remain healthy early in progression

[H2]:
- Hypothesis: Nutrient deficiency
- Expected evidence:
  - Repeatable discoloration pattern across comparable leaves
  - Possible growth effects beyond discrete lesions
- Contradicting evidence:
  - Concentric lesions are not explained by deficiency alone

[H3]:
- Hypothesis: Root-zone water stress
- Expected evidence:
  - Moisture extremes or poor drainage
  - Wilting or generalized growth stress
- Contradicting evidence:
  - Standing water is not observed

[CandidateCauses]:
- [H1]
- [H2]
- [H3]

[EvidenceStatus]:
- Observed: Facts directly recorded in [ObservedEvidence]
- Inferred: Relationships suggested by a candidate cause
- Unknown: Evidence not yet collected

[Assessment]: []

[NextChecks]: []

[Unresolved]: []

[CompareHypotheses]:
=> For each hypothesis in [CandidateCauses] for [Plant] ↳
  - Match its expected evidence against [ObservedEvidence]
  - Record supporting observations as observed evidence
  - Record contradicting observations as observed evidence
  - Mark missing diagnostic information as unknown
  - Classify each item using [EvidenceStatus]
  - Do not convert a correlation into an observed fact
=> Append the comparison to [Assessment]

[ChooseChecks]:
=> Identify checks that can distinguish the highest-support candidate causes
=> Prefer low-risk checks before treatment
=> If [H1] remains plausible:
  - Inspect additional older leaves for the same lesion pattern
  - Record whether lesions expanded after another wet period
=> If [H2] remains plausible:
  - Compare symptom distribution across old and new growth
  - Review available soil or tissue test evidence before adding nutrients
=> If [H3] remains plausible:
  - Check moisture at multiple root-zone locations
  - Check drainage after irrigation or rain
=> Append required observations to [NextChecks]

[Conclude]:
=> Rank candidate causes by current evidence support
=> Label each ranked cause as supported possible or weakly supported
=> If evidence is insufficient to distinguish leading causes:
  - Preserve them in [Unresolved]
  - Recommend the relevant [NextChecks]
=> If symptoms progress rapidly or the plant declines despite reversible corrections:
  - Recommend review by a local plant diagnostic service
=> Do not recommend a cause-specific treatment as certain unless the evidence justifies that level of confidence

Task:
1. Run [CompareHypotheses]
2. Run [ChooseChecks]
3. Run [Conclude]
4. Present [Assessment] with [NextChecks] and [Unresolved]
