# Intent: Design a seasonal garden plan that coordinates crop windows with bed capacity succession and site constraints

[SeasonAssumptions]:
- Source: ./local-growing-calendar.md
- LastExpectedFrost: 15 April
- FirstExpectedFrost: 1 November
- SummerHeatPeriod: 1 July through 31 August

[Site]:
- Sun exposure: Full sun
- Irrigation: Drip available in every bed
- Bed A:
  - Area: 3.6 m²
  - Spring soil condition: Workable
- Bed B:
  - Area: 3.6 m²
  - Spring soil condition: Workable
- Bed C:
  - Area: 3.6 m²
  - Spring soil condition: Workable

[Crops]:
- Pea:
  - Planting window: 1 March through 31 March
  - Expected bed release: 20 June through 10 July
  - Season preference: Cool
- Tomato:
  - Transplant window: 1 May through 20 May
  - Expected bed release: 1 October through 20 October
  - Season preference: Warm
- BushBean:
  - Planting window: 15 May through 15 July
  - Expected bed release: 55 through 70 days after planting
  - Season preference: Warm
- Lettuce:
  - Spring window: 15 March through 15 April
  - Fall window: 20 August through 15 September
  - Season preference: Cool
- Garlic:
  - Planting window: 15 October through 15 November
  - Expected bed release: Following June
  - Season preference: Overwintering
- CoverCrop:
  - Planting window: After final food crop
  - Purpose:
    - Protect bare soil
    - Hold unused bed capacity

[RotationConstraints]:
- Transition:
  - From: Tomato
  - To: Tomato
  - Rule: Avoid consecutive occupancy in the same bed
- Transition:
  - From: Pea
  - To: BushBean
  - Rule: Allowed when the pea crop is removed before bean establishment
- Transition:
  - From: FoodCrop
  - To: CoverCrop
  - Rule: Allowed whenever no planned successor can establish in time
- Transition:
  - From: AnyCrop
  - To: Successor
  - Rule: Preserve enough turnover time for residue removal and bed preparation

[Conflicts]: []

[BuildWindows]:
=> For each crop in [Crops] ↳
  - Map its planting window against [SeasonAssumptions]
  - Record its expected bed occupancy
  - Mark heat or frost exposure that could make the window unreliable
=> Preserve source-based uncertainty rather than inventing exact harvest dates

[AllocateBeds]:
=> Assign crops to [Site] beds without overlapping bed occupancy
=> Prefer successions where a released bed can receive the next crop inside its planting window
=> Apply [RotationConstraints] before optimizing bed utilization
=> If two crops compete for the same bed window:
  - Preserve the crop with the narrower viable window
  - Search another bed or later succession for the other crop
  - Append any unresolved competition to [Conflicts]
=> Append accepted assignments to [SeasonPlan]

[PlanTurnover]:
=> For each transition in [SeasonPlan] ↳
  - Calculate the available gap between expected release and successor planting
  - Reserve time for residue removal and bed preparation
  - If the successor window cannot tolerate the required turnover gap:
    - Append the transition to [TurnoverGaps]
  - If a bed would otherwise remain bare for a material interval:
    - Evaluate [Crops] CoverCrop against the remaining window
    - If cover crop establishment is not feasible:
      - Append the uncovered interval to [BareSoilIntervals]
=> Do not claim a succession is viable when its timing depends on an unresolved release date

[ValidatePlan]:
=> Verify every bed assignment fits within its seasonal window
=> Verify no bed contains overlapping occupancy
=> Verify [RotationConstraints] are preserved
=> Verify every planned successor has a feasible turnover interval or a recorded gap
=> Verify bare-soil intervals are either covered or explicitly recorded
=> Preserve weather-sensitive timing as an assumption rather than a guarantee

Task:
1. Run [BuildWindows]
2. Run [AllocateBeds]
3. Run [PlanTurnover]
4. Run [ValidatePlan]
5. Provide [SeasonPlan] with:
  - [TurnoverGaps]
  - [BareSoilIntervals]
  - [Conflicts]
