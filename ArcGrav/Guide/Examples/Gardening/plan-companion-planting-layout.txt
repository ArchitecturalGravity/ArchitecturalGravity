# Intent: Arrange a companion planting layout that respects crop compatibility spatial competition site conditions and garden objectives

[GardenObjective]:
- Primary: Support reliable vegetable production
- Secondary:
  - Improve pollinator access
  - Reduce bare soil
  - Preserve harvest access

[Site]:
- Bed: 1.2 m × 3.6 m raised bed
- Hemisphere: Northern
- Orientation: Long axis east to west
- Sun exposure:
  - South edge: Full sun
  - North edge: Full sun with late afternoon fence shade
- Irrigation: Two drip lines running east to west
- Access: Reach from both long edges must remain open

[Plants]:
- Tomato:
  - Quantity: 2
  - Mature spacing: 60 cm
  - Light need: Full sun
  - Root competition: High
  - Support: Trellis required
- BushBean:
  - Quantity: 6
  - Mature spacing: 20 cm
  - Light need: Full sun
  - Root competition: Moderate
- Lettuce:
  - Quantity: 8
  - Mature spacing: 20 cm
  - Light need: Tolerates partial shade
  - Root competition: Low
- Basil:
  - Quantity: 4
  - Mature spacing: 25 cm
  - Light need: Full sun
  - Root competition: Low
- Calendula:
  - Quantity: 3
  - Mature spacing: 30 cm
  - Objective role: Pollinator support

[CompatibilityRules]:
- Source: ./crop-compatibility-notes.md
- Pairing:
  - Between:
    - Tomato
    - Basil
  - Rule: Preferred nearby pairing
- Pairing:
  - Between:
    - Tomato
    - Lettuce
  - Rule: Allowed outside the tomato root-dense zone
- Pairing:
  - Between:
    - Tomato
    - BushBean
  - Rule: Allowed when mature spacing is preserved
- Pairing:
  - Between:
    - Calendula
    - VegetableCrops
  - Rule: Place within the bed without blocking harvest access
- GeneralRule:
  - Applies to: AnyPair
  - Rule: Override a preferred pairing when required spacing or light access would be violated

[SpatialRules]:
- Trellised tomatoes: Place on the north side to reduce shading of shorter crops
- Drip access: Every plant must be reachable by a drip line or emitter
- Harvest access: No mature canopy may require crossing the center of the bed for routine harvest
- Edge clearance: Keep 15 cm clear from the bed frame unless a plant is intentionally edge-planted

[MapConstraints]:
=> Divide [Site] into plantable zones around the two drip lines
=> Reserve tomato spacing and trellis clearance before assigning smaller crops
=> Mark areas where tomato shade is expected to increase later in the season
=> Mark reachable harvest zones from both long edges
=> Append the result to [CandidatePositions]

[PlaceCompanions]:
=> Place tomato anchors first under [SpatialRules]
=> For each remaining plant in [Plants] ↳
  - Find positions that satisfy its mature spacing
  - Check the position against [CompatibilityRules]
  - Check light need against expected shade
  - Check irrigation access
  - Prefer positions that advance [GardenObjective] secondary goals without violating hard constraints
  - If no valid position exists:
    - Append an unplaced record with the plant and blocking constraints to [Conflicts]
=> If a preferred companion relationship conflicts with spacing light or access:
  - Preserve the hard constraint
  - Record the rejected relationship in [Conflicts]
=> Append accepted positions to [Layout]

[ValidateLayout]:
=> Verify every plant in [Plants] has the requested quantity or an explicit unplaced record
=> Verify no pair violates mature spacing
=> Verify every position has compatible light and irrigation access
=> Verify the tomato trellis does not create an avoidable shade conflict for full-sun crops
=> Verify routine harvest access remains open
=> Preserve every unresolved placement conflict in [Conflicts]

Task:
1. Run [MapConstraints]
2. Run [PlaceCompanions]
3. Run [ValidateLayout]
