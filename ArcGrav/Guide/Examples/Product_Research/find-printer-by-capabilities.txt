# Intent: Find discontinued or older monochrome laser printers that meet the required capabilities with documented evidence

[Product]:
- Required:
  - Monochrome laser printer
  - Integrated scanner
  - Introduced in 2019 or earlier
- Permitted:
  - Discontinued models

[Capability]:
- Required:
  - Print from USB drive
  - Scan to USB drive
  - Full duplex printing
- Permitted:
  - Single-sided scanning
- Not a selection criterion:
  - Print speed

[EvidencePriority]:
P0. Manufacturer user manuals
P1. Manufacturer specification sheets
P2. Archived manufacturer product pages
P3. Secondary product sources for discovery only when primary documentation is unavailable

[FoundProducts]: []

[Confirmed]: []

[EvidenceGaps]: []

[Search]:
=> Search for [Product]
=> Append candidate models and discovery sources to [FoundProducts]

[ReviewConfirmation]:
=> Observe [FoundProducts]
=> For each candidate verify all required entries in [Product] and [Capability] against [EvidencePriority]
=> Distinguish the presence of a USB port from documented print from USB drive and scan to USB drive support
=> Treat unavailable or conflicting product or capability evidence as unverified
=> Append candidates with every required entry verified to [Confirmed]
=> Append missing or conflicting evidence to [EvidenceGaps]

Task:
1. Run [Search]
2. Run [ReviewConfirmation]
3. Provide [Confirmed] with product and capability evidence and [EvidenceGaps]
