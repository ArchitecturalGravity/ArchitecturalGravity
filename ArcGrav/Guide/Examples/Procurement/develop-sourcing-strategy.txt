# Intent: Develop a sourcing strategy that connects demand profile supply market conditions qualification dependencies commercial levers and continuity objectives

[SourcingContext]:
- Scope: Food-grade stainless pump assemblies
- Planning horizon: 36 months
- Current state: One approved production source
- Current agreement: Expires 2027-03-31

[Objectives]:
- Protect production continuity during demand growth
- Reduce exposure to single-source interruption
- Maintain validated product performance
- Improve commercial transparency before contract renewal
- Avoid qualification activity that cannot complete before the renewal decision

[DemandPortfolio]:
- CoreProduction:
  - Forecast share: 82 percent
  - Demand pattern: Stable monthly consumption
  - Service consequence: Line stop if unavailable
- GrowthProgram:
  - Forecast share: 13 percent
  - Demand pattern: Ramp begins in 2027 Q3
  - Service consequence: Launch delay if unavailable
- EngineeringUse:
  - Forecast share: 5 percent
  - Demand pattern: Irregular small lots
  - Service consequence: Development delay if unavailable

[SupplyMarket]:
- QualifiedIncumbent:
  - Status: Approved for all production use
  - Constraint: Limited surge capacity during regional peak season
- CandidateRegionalSource:
  - Status: Commercially capable
  - Dependency: Product validation required before production award
- CandidateOffshoreSource:
  - Status: Technical screening complete
  - Dependencies:
    - Import lead time validation required
    - Traceability audit required
- MarketCondition:
  - Raw material pricing follows published nickel index
  - Machining capacity is available from multiple suppliers
  - Specialized sanitary welding capacity is concentrated

[Dependencies]:
- Dependency:
  - Dependent: Alternate source production award
  - Requires: Successful product validation
- Dependency:
  - Dependent: Successful product validation
  - Requires: Approved pilot units
- Dependency:
  - Dependent: Approved pilot units
  - Requires: Final drawing package
- Dependency:
  - Dependent: Split award at renewal
  - Requires: Alternate source approval before 2027-03-31

[StrategyOptions]:
- RenewSingleSource:
  - Structure: Renew incumbent for full production demand
  - Advantage: No qualification transition
  - Exposure: Single-source continuity risk remains
- QualifyRegionalDualSource:
  - Structure: Retain incumbent and qualify regional alternate
  - Advantage: Creates recoverable alternate capacity
  - Exposure: Validation must finish before split award
- QualifyOffshoreDualSource:
  - Structure: Retain incumbent and qualify offshore alternate
  - Advantage: Expands commercial competition
  - Exposure: Longer replenishment path increases inventory dependency

[CommercialLevers]:
- Indexed raw material adjustment with defined reference
- Open-book treatment for extraordinary expedite cost
- Capacity reservation tied to forecast bands
- Tooling ownership and maintenance responsibility
- Volume allocation ranges rather than guaranteed fixed share

[DecisionCriteria]:
- Continuity resilience
- Qualification feasibility
- Total landed cost behavior
- Capacity scalability
- Inventory consequence
- Negotiating leverage

[Actions]: []

[Assess]:
=> For each option in [StrategyOptions] ↳
  => Trace required conditions through [Dependencies]
  => Check timing constraints against [SourcingContext]
  => Evaluate fit to each segment in [DemandPortfolio]
  => Test assumptions against [SupplyMarket]
  => Identify risks that cannot be mitigated by contract terms alone
  => Identify useful levers from [CommercialLevers] only when the option remains viable

[Choose]:
=> Compare viable options against [Objectives] and [DecisionCriteria]
=> Prefer an option only when its qualification path can support the renewal decision
=> Do not treat lower quoted piece price as lower total cost without considering inventory and transition effects
=> Preserve unresolved market assumptions that require supplier engagement
=> Append the selected direction and decision rationale to [Strategy]
=> Append only actions required to establish or execute [Strategy] to [Actions]

Task:
