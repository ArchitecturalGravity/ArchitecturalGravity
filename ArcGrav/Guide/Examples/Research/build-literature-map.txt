# Intent: Build a literature map of urban-heat mitigation research without flattening the field into a single narrative summary by exposing:
#   - Major intervention families
#   - Methods
#   - Claim dependencies
#   - Evidence gaps

[Scope]:
- Question: How do neighborhood-scale interventions reduce outdoor heat exposure, and where is the evidence strong enough to generalize
- Include: Empirical studies of the following interventions:
  - Tree canopy
  - Shade structures
  - Cool roofs
  - Cool pavements
  - Combined interventions
- Exclude: Purely indoor cooling interventions and papers without a measured or modeled outdoor thermal outcome
- Period: 2010-present

[Corpus]:
- ./bibliography.ris
- ./papers/
- ./screening-notes.csv

[MapDimensions]:
- Intervention family
- Climate and urban form
- Study design
- Spatial scale
- Thermal outcome measured
- Exposure or equity outcome measured
- Causal versus descriptive claim
- Replication or direct contradiction

[Clusters]: []

[Relations]: []

[Gaps]: []

[Classify]:
=> Screen [Corpus] against [Scope]
=> For each included study:
  => Record the study against [MapDimensions], including:
    - Primary intervention
    - Setting
    - Design
    - Outcome
    - Strongest supported claim
  => Assign it to one or more coherent [Clusters] derived from the literature rather than a predetermined taxonomy when necessary

[Connect]:
=> Within and across [Clusters] ↳
  => Record when one study materially:
    - Replicates another study's claim
    - Extends another study's claim
    - Qualifies another study's claim
    - Contradicts another study's claim
  => Record when a later result depends on an inherited:
    - Measurement method
    - Model
    - Dataset
    - Assumption
  => Append these relationships to [Relations]
=> Do not infer intellectual influence from chronology alone when citation or methodological dependence is absent

[MapGaps]:
=> Identify claims supported mainly by one:
  - Climate
  - City type
  - Method
  - Research group
=> Identify intervention comparisons that are commonly discussed but rarely tested head-to-head
=> Identify population or equity outcomes that are asserted more often than measured
=> Separate true evidence gaps from topics excluded by [Scope]
=> Append the resulting gaps to [Gaps]

Task:
1. Run [Classify]
2. Run [Connect]
3. Run [MapGaps]
4. Present:
  - Literature map organized by clusters and relations
  - Most consequential evidence gaps
  - Scope limits of the map
