# Intent: Synthesize conflicting evidence about whether four-day workweeks improve organizational outcomes without averaging unlike results by reconciling differences in:
#   - Study design
#   - Implementation
#   - Outcome definition

[Question]: Under what conditions does a four-day workweek lead to:
  - Improved organizational performance and employee well-being
  - Preserved organizational performance and employee well-being
  - Reduced organizational performance and employee well-being

[Sources]:
- ./controlled-trials/
- ./pilot-evaluations/
- ./observational-studies/
- ./systematic-reviews/
- ./study-characteristics.csv

[ConflictFrame]:
- Intervention:
  - Reduced hours with unchanged pay
  - Compressed hours
  - Ambiguous
- Comparator:
  - Prior schedule
  - Concurrent control
  - External benchmark
- Duration:
  - Short pilot
  - Medium term
  - Long term
- Outcome:
  - Output
  - Revenue
  - Service quality
  - Absence
  - Retention
  - Self-reported well-being
- Implementation:
  - Workload reduced
  - Workload unchanged
  - Process redesigned
  - Not reported

[Findings]: []

[Synthesize]:
=> Extract each material finding from [Sources] relevant to [Question] ↳
  - Study design
  - Sample
  - Intervention form
  - Comparator
  - Duration
  - Outcome definition
=> Group apparently conflicting findings by the part of [ConflictFrame] that could explain the disagreement
=> Distinguish conflicts in measured results from conflicts created by different definitions of "four-day workweek" or "performance"
=> Give greater inferential weight to designs that better address the claim being evaluated, while preserving relevant external-validity limits
=> Append to [Findings] ↳
  - Conclusion supported
  - Evidence basis
  - Conditions under which it holds
  - Contradictory evidence that remains material
  - Confidence and principal limitation

[Reconcile]:
=> Identify disagreements that are substantially resolved by differences in:
  - Intervention type
  - Sector
  - Time horizon
  - Outcome definition
=> Identify disagreements that remain after those differences are accounted for
=> Do not treat self-reported well-being as a proxy for organizational performance or vice versa
=> Do not generalize from voluntary pilots to mandatory organization-wide adoption without noting selection effects
=> State where the evidence supports conditional conclusions rather than a universal effect

Task:
1. Run [Synthesize]
2. Run [Reconcile]
3. Present:
  - Conditional findings
  - Reconciled disagreements
  - Genuinely unresolved conflicts
  - Study characteristics most important for interpreting future evidence
