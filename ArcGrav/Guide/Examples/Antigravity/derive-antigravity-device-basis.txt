# Intent: Derive the strongest defensible conceptual basis for an antigravity-related device by separating established physics from speculative mechanisms and identifying what evidence would be required before device formation

[Question]:
- What physically meaningful mechanism, if any, could support formation of a device intended to:
  - Reduce apparent weight
  - Alter inertial response
  - Produce a gravity-like effect

[SourceClasses]:
P0. Highest-authority sources:
  - Peer-reviewed experimental results
  - Established physical theory
P1. Reproducible laboratory observations with documented controls
P2. Credible theoretical proposals with explicit assumptions and testable predictions
P3. Lower-confidence source classes:
  - Unreplicated claims
  - Anecdotal reports
  - Speculative interpretations

[Phenomena]:
- Apparent weight reduction
- Sustained force without conventional reaction mass
- Inertial modification
- Spacetime or gravitational-field manipulation
- Ordinary effects that can imitate gravitational anomalies:
  - Electromagnetic
  - Acoustic
  - Fluid
  - Mechanical
  - Measurement

[KnownAlternatives]:
- Buoyancy
- Aerodynamic lift
- Magnetic force
- Electrostatic force
- Acoustic radiation pressure
- Mechanical vibration
- Thermal gradients
- Ion wind
- Sensor drift
- Center-of-mass shift
- Ordinary reaction forces transmitted through:
  - Supports
  - Wiring

[DerivedBasis]: []

[RejectedClaims]: []

[Unresolved]: []

[RequiredEvidence]: []

[Derive]:
=> For each candidate mechanism found in [SourceClasses] ↳
  1. Identify the governing claim
  2. Identify the targeted effects in [Phenomena]
  3. Classify the claim as established | supported | speculative | unsupported
  4. Record the physical quantities the mechanism predicts should change
  5. Compare the predicted observation against [KnownAlternatives]
  6. Identify assumptions required for the mechanism to produce a device-scale effect
  7. Preserve source provenance and uncertainty
=> Append mechanisms with defensible, testable structure to [DerivedBasis]
=> Append contradicted or evidentially unsupported mechanisms to [RejectedClaims]
=> Append unresolved mechanisms to [Unresolved]
=> For each unresolved or speculative mechanism, append the minimum discriminating evidence to [RequiredEvidence]

Constraint:
- Do not treat the existence of practical antigravity as established
- Do not convert unexplained force measurements into gravitational effects without excluding ordinary forces
- Do not infer engineering feasibility from theoretical possibility alone
- Keep these categories explicitly separated:
  - Established physics
  - Speculative theory
  - Experimental anomaly claims
- Preserve null results and contradictory evidence
- Exclude hazardous fabrication parameters or construction instructions

[FormationGate]:
=> A mechanism may proceed to conceptual device formation only when it has:
  - A clearly defined effect
  - A measurable input-output relation or qualitative prediction
  - At least one discriminating test against ordinary-force explanations
  - Explicit assumptions
  - Known failure interpretations

Task:
1. Resolve current authoritative sources relevant to [Question]
2. Run [Derive]
3. Apply [FormationGate] to [DerivedBasis]
4. Return:
  - Derived candidate mechanisms
  - Rejected claims and why they fail
  - Unresolved mechanisms
  - Required evidence
  - Which mechanisms, if any, are suitable for conceptual device formation
