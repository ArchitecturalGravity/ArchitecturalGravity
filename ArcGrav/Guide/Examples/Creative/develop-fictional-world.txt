# Intent: Develop a coherent fictional world from a speculative premise by tracing how its rules shape
# - Institutions
# - Daily life
# - Conflict
# - Story possibilities

[Premise]:
- A chain of inhabited islands drifts continuously across a planet-wide ocean
- Islands periodically converge into temporary archipelagos before separating again
- Long-distance navigation is reliable, but no island can permanently anchor itself

[WorldRules]:
- Preserve the physical premise rather than explaining it away
- Treat movement as an ordinary fact of life for inhabitants
- Let geography create both opportunities and costs
- Prefer consequences that can generate stories over decorative lore

[World]: []

[Develop]:
=> Establish the practical consequences of [Premise] for:
  - Settlement and architecture
  - Food supply
  - Water supply
  - Material supply
  - Travel and communication
  - Government and jurisdiction
  - Trade and currency
  - Family
  - Identity
  - Belonging
=> For each consequence:
  => Trace at least one second-order effect
  => Identify who benefits and who bears the cost
  => Add the resulting world element to [World]

[PressurePoints]:
=> Observe [World]
=> Identify tensions created when islands converge or separate
=> Prefer tensions that arise naturally from:
  - Incompatible needs
  - Timing
  - Scarcity
  - Law
  - Relationships
=> Derive three recurring conflicts that could support stories at different scales:
  - Personal
  - Community
  - Regional

[ConsistencyCheck]:
=> Test each major element in [World] against [Premise] and [WorldRules]
=> Remove assumptions that depend on fixed geography unless the world supplies a plausible substitute
=> Keep unresolved mysteries unresolved when an answer is not necessary for coherence

Task:
1. Run [Develop]
2. Run [PressurePoints]
3. Run [ConsistencyCheck]
4. Present a concise world brief containing:
  - Governing rules
  - Major institutions
  - Everyday consequences
  - Story-generating tensions
