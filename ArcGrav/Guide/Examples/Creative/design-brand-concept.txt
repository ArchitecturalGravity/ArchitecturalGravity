# Intent: Design a distinctive brand concept from a product brief by
# - Exploring meaningfully different creative territories
# - Selecting one against the brief
# - Translating it into usable verbal and visual direction

[Brief]:
- Product: Neighborhood tool-lending membership
- Audience: Apartment dwellers and first-time homeowners who need tools occasionally
- Promise: Access to the right tool without:
  - Buying it
  - Storing it
  - Maintaining it
- Personality:
  - Capable
  - Neighborly
  - Practical
  - Optimistic
- Launch context: One dense urban neighborhood before wider expansion

[Avoid]:
- Construction-industry toughness
- Rustic workshop nostalgia
- Sustainability claims that cannot be substantiated
- Language that implies tools or membership are free

[Territories]: []

[Explore]:
=> Create three concept territories that interpret [Brief] from substantially different angles
=> For each territory, define:
  - Central idea
  - Emotional benefit
  - Naming direction
  - Voice characteristics
  - Visual principles
  - One risk or likely failure mode
=> Reject territories that depend on an item in [Avoid]
=> Append the viable territories to [Territories]

[Choose]:
=> Compare [Territories] for:
  - Distinctiveness
  - Clarity of the product promise
  - Fit with the audience
  - Ability to scale beyond the launch neighborhood
=> Prefer a concept that can be expressed without explaining the business model in every message
=> Select one territory and state why it is stronger than the alternatives

[ConceptSystem]:
- Brand idea
- Positioning statement
- Naming principles with 5 candidate names
- Voice: 3 traits with do/don't guidance
- Visual direction:
  - Typography principles
  - Shape principles
  - Imagery principles
  - Composition principles
- Homepage headline and subhead
- Membership call to action
- Three example messages for common member moments

Task:
1. Run [Explore]
2. Run [Choose]
3. Develop [ConceptSystem] from the selected territory
4. Present the selected concept and enough rationale to show how it follows [Brief]
