[DecisionRules]:
- Treat a credible task-blocking accessibility failure as higher priority than a more frequent cosmetic or low-impact problem
- Do not convert weak evidence into high confidence by assigning a numerical score
- Use frequency to distinguish findings with comparable impact and evidence strength
- Prefer an independent ready fix when a higher-impact finding is temporarily blocked
- Send a finding to further research when the main barrier is evidence quality rather than delivery capacity
- Preserve blocked high-impact findings in the queue rather than downgrading their user impact

[Prioritize]:

  - Identify any independent research or design work that can proceed
