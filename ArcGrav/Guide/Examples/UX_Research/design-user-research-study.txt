# Intent: Design a moderated user research study that produces decision-ready evidence about whether first-time workspace setup is understandable without leading participants or collecting unnecessary personal data

Scope: Harbor Team Workspace pre-release usability study

[StudyContext]:
- Product: Harbor Team Workspace
- Product stage: Pre-release prototype
- Decision: Determine whether the setup flow is ready for a broader beta
- Study window: Two weeks
- Session format: Remote moderated usability study

[Q1]: Can first-time users explain what a workspace represents before creating one

[Q2]: Can first-time users complete setup without moderator assistance

[Q3]: Which labels or decisions create hesitation during setup

[Q4]: Do users understand what will be shared with invited teammates

[ResearchQuestions]:
- [Q1]
- [Q2]
- [Q3]
- [Q4]

[ParticipantCriteria]:
- Required:
  - Uses collaborative software at least weekly
  - Has joined or created a shared digital workspace within the last year
  - Is comfortable sharing a screen during a remote session
- Mix:
  - Individual contributors
  - People who coordinate small teams
  - People with prior experience configuring collaboration tools
  - People without prior experience configuring collaboration tools
- Exclude:
  - Current Harbor employees or contractors
  - Participants involved in designing the prototype
  - Participants who cannot use the prototype without accessibility support the prototype does not yet provide

[Recruitment]:
- Target: 8 completed sessions
- Reserve: 2 backup participants
- Compensation: Fixed study incentive communicated before scheduling
- Screening rule: Collect only information needed to evaluate [ParticipantCriteria]

[Method]:
- Opening:
  - Explain that the product is being evaluated rather than the participant
  - Obtain consent for note taking and session recording
  - Confirm that the participant may stop at any time
- Scenario:
  - Ask the participant to imagine joining a new cross-functional project
  - Ask the participant to create a workspace for that project
  - Ask the participant to invite two teammates using provided test accounts
- Moderator conduct:
  - Ask neutral follow-up questions after observable hesitation
  - Do not explain interface labels before the participant interprets them
  - Do not rescue a failed task until the failure state is recorded
  - Do not ask for personal credentials or real contact information
- Closing:
  - Ask the participant to describe what they believe teammates can see
  - Ask which setup decision felt least clear
  - Ask what they would expect to happen next

[EvidencePlan]:
- Question [Q1] ↳
  - Primary evidence: Participant explanation before workspace creation
  - Supporting evidence: First navigation choice
- Question [Q2] ↳
  - Primary evidence: Task completion without moderator assistance
  - Supporting evidence:
    - Recovery from wrong turns
    - Time spent at blocking decisions
- Question [Q3] ↳
  - Primary evidence:
    - Observable hesitation
    - Repeated reading
    - Backtracking
  - Supporting evidence: Participant explanation after the task
- Question [Q4] ↳
  - Primary evidence: Participant description of teammate visibility
  - Supporting evidence: Invite configuration choices

[SessionRecord]:
- Participant code
- Screening attributes relevant to [ParticipantCriteria]
- Task outcome
- Moderator assistance events
- Observation notes with timestamps
- Participant statements with timestamps
- Recording reference when consent exists
- Researcher interpretation kept separate from direct observation

[StudyConstraints]:
- Use test accounts only
- Do not collect protected personal characteristics unless independently required for the research decision
- Do not treat completion time alone as evidence of understanding
- Preserve failed attempts and confusion rather than editing them out of the record
- Report accessibility barriers observed during the study without generalizing beyond the tested conditions
