# Intent: Build a multi-city trip around these conditions without overpacking the itinerary
#   - Fixed arrival and departure points
#   - Timed commitments
#   - Transfer constraints
#   - Traveler preferences

[Trip]:
- Two adults traveling for 10 nights
- Arrive Rome Fiumicino on 2027-05-08 at 09:10
- Depart Venice Marco Polo on 2027-05-18 at 12:20
- Travel between cities by rail unless another mode materially improves reliability
- Desired cities:
  - Rome
  - Florence
  - Bologna
  - Venice

[FixedCommitments]:
- Florence museum reservation: 2027-05-12 at 10:30
- Bologna food tour: 2027-05-15 at 17:00
- Final night must be in Venice

[TravelPreferences]:
- No scheduled sightseeing on arrival day before 15:00
- Avoid changing hotels for a one-night stay unless it prevents a much longer transfer
- Keep at least one unstructured half-day in each city with two or more nights
- Prefer direct rail legs under 3 hours
- Do not schedule a timed attraction within 2 hours of an intercity arrival
- Limit each day to two major planned activities

[Sources]:
- Current rail timetables and reservation requirements
- Official attraction opening hours and entry rules
- Airport-to-city and city-to-airport transport options
- Accommodation check-in and baggage-storage policies when relevant

[Itinerary]: []

[Route]:
=> Place [FixedCommitments] on the timeline defined by [Trip] first
=> Determine the minimum nights needed in each city to satisfy those commitments without violating [TravelPreferences] ↳
  - Florence
  - Bologna
  - Venice
=> Allocate remaining nights to reduce backtracking and excessive hotel changes
=> For each city transition:
  => Verify a feasible transport option from [Sources]
  => Include station-to-lodging transfer time where it could affect a timed commitment
  => Add recovery margin for ordinary delay
  => Reject a transfer that leaves less than 2 hours before a fixed timed activity
=> Append the resulting city sequence and night allocation to [Itinerary]

[FillDays]:
=> For each city in [Itinerary] ↳
  => Preserve [FixedCommitments]
  => Group optional activities by neighborhood or natural route
  => Add no more than two major planned activities per day
  => Preserve the required unstructured half-day where applicable
  => Keep arrival and departure periods lighter than full sightseeing days
=> Do not fill free time merely because capacity remains

[Resilience]:
=> Identify the intercity leg with the greatest consequence if delayed
=> For that leg:
  => Prefer an earlier departure when it materially protects a fixed commitment
  => Record one fallback service or same-day alternative when available
=> Verify the airport transfer for departure morning separately from ordinary city transport
=> If a planned stop creates disproportionate transfer overhead or forces a one-night stay:
  => Remove that stop before compressing recovery time elsewhere

Task:
1. Run [Route]
2. Run [FillDays]
3. Apply [Resilience]
4. Present:
  - Dated city-by-city itinerary
  - Night allocation
  - Major activities
  - Rail legs
  - Transfer buffers
  - Free-time blocks
  - Most important fallback option
