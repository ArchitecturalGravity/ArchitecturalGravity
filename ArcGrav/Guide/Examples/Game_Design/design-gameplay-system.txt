# Intent: Design a cooperative stealth gameplay system whose alert economy creates readable escalation while forcing meaningful resource choices across progression without removing recovery opportunities

[GameContext]:
- Mode: Two-player cooperative infiltration
- Session target: 25 to 35 minutes
- Player fantasy: Skilled infiltrators adapting under pressure
- Failure model: Escalating opposition before mission failure

[DesignGoals]:
- Reward observation before commitment
- Make noisy shortcuts useful without making stealth irrelevant
- Let partners recover from small mistakes through coordinated play
- Make higher alert states change decisions rather than only increase enemy health
- Keep progression focused on new options rather than permanent numerical dominance

[CoreLoop]:
1. Scout patrols and security devices
2. Choose a route or create an opening
3. Spend limited tools to control risk
4. Complete an objective action
5. Respond to resulting alert changes
6. Reach extraction before alert pressure becomes unmanageable

[Resources]:
- Focus:
  - Each player starts with 3 charges
  - One charge refreshes after completing a mission objective without raising alert
  - Maximum capacity is 4 charges
  - Uses:
    - Mark one patrol route for both players
    - Perform a silent synchronized takedown
    - Accelerate a partner recovery action
- BreachCharge:
  - Team starts with 2 charges
  - Opens reinforced shortcuts
  - Always adds 2 alert
  - Cannot be replenished during a mission
- CoverIdentity:
  - Each player starts with 1 use
  - Cancels the first suspicion event while alert is Quiet or Suspicious
  - Fails automatically during Lockdown

[AlertState]:
- Quiet:
  - Alert range: 0 to 1
  - Guards follow normal patrols
  - Cameras use standard sweep speed
- Suspicious:
  - Alert range: 2 to 3
  - Guards investigate recent disturbance locations
  - Cameras pause longer on detected movement
  - CoverIdentity remains usable
- Searching:
  - Alert range: 4 to 5
  - Reinforcement patrols enter from security posts
  - Objective interactions take 25 percent longer unless both players cooperate
  - CoverIdentity is disabled
- Lockdown:
  - Alert range: 6
  - Extraction route changes to the emergency exit
  - Reinforcement patrols continue until alert is reduced
  - Mission timer loses 90 seconds when Lockdown begins

[AlertRules]:
- Raising alert:
  - Unsuppressed weapon fire adds 2
  - A confirmed player sighting adds 2
  - A BreachCharge adds 2
  - A disabled camera discovered by a guard adds 1
- Reducing alert:
  - Completing a cooperative concealment action removes 1
  - Reaching a safe room while no player is pursued removes 1
  - Alert cannot fall below Suspicious after Lockdown has occurred
- Bounds:
  - Alert cannot exceed 6
  - Alert changes resolve after the triggering action completes

[InteractionRules]:
- A BreachCharge may shorten the route while increasing the chance of entering Searching
- Spending Focus on scouting may reveal a longer route with fewer alert triggers
- Saving Focus improves recovery capacity after an unexpected detection
- Entering Lockdown converts route knowledge into time pressure because extraction changes
- Cooperative objective interactions trade positional safety for faster completion during Searching

[Progression]:
- Mission success grants 1 upgrade token
- Upgrade categories:
  - Recon:
    - Reveal one additional patrol segment when Focus is spent on marking
  - Coordination:
    - Reduce synchronized takedown Focus cost for one player once per mission
  - Recovery:
    - Restore one CoverIdentity use after completing the second mission objective
- Progression limits:
  - No upgrade increases weapon damage
  - No upgrade removes alert generation from noisy actions
  - No upgrade bypasses Lockdown extraction rules

[Scenarios]:
- Clean infiltration:
  - Players scout before moving
  - Players complete two objectives without confirmed sightings
  - Expected pressure: Focus partially refreshes while alert remains recoverable
- Fast breach:
  - Players spend both BreachCharge uses to shorten traversal
  - Expected pressure: Route time decreases while alert approaches Searching or Lockdown
- Recovery after detection:
  - One player is confirmed by a guard at alert 2
  - Expected pressure: Alert reaches Searching and the team must choose between concealment or continued objective pressure
- Late Lockdown:
  - Lockdown begins after the final objective
  - Expected pressure: Extraction reroutes and the reduced timer makes stored route knowledge valuable

[DesignReview]: []

[Evaluate]:
=> Trace [Scenarios] through [CoreLoop] using:
  - [GameContext]
  - [Resources]
  - [AlertState]
  - [AlertRules]
  - [InteractionRules]
=> Record any dominant strategy that removes a meaningful route or resource decision
=> Record any state transition that lacks a player-readable consequence
=> Check whether [Progression] expands decisions without invalidating the base alert economy
=> Preserve tensions that support [DesignGoals] instead of flattening every option to equal value
=> Append required rule adjustments and unresolved design questions to [DesignReview]

[Validate]:
=> Verify every resource in [Resources] has a meaningful scarcity or opportunity cost
=> Verify every state in [AlertState] changes player-facing decisions
=> Verify at least one recovery path remains after ordinary detection
=> Verify Lockdown is materially different from lower alert states
=> Verify progression cannot permanently erase the consequences of noisy play

Task:
1. Run [Evaluate]
2. Run [Validate]
3. Present [DesignReview] with the interaction or state relationship that justifies each proposed adjustment
