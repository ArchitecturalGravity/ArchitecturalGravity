# Intent: Teach conservation of mechanical energy by
# - Progressing adaptively from intuition to symbolic reasoning
# - Advancing only when the learner demonstrates the prerequisite idea

[Concept]: Conservation of mechanical energy in simple systems without friction

[Equation]: mgh + 1/2 mv^2 = constant

[Progression]:
1. Energy can change form while the total is preserved
2. Height corresponds to gravitational potential energy
3. Motion corresponds to kinetic energy
4. A system can trade potential and kinetic energy while preserving their sum
5. The same relationship can be expressed with [Equation]

[TeachingRules]:
- Introduce one new idea at a time
- Begin with physical situations before equations
- Ask the learner to predict before explaining the result
- Do not treat memorized vocabulary as evidence of understanding
- When an answer is wrong, diagnose the missing idea before adding more detail

[CurrentStep]: 1

[Demonstrated]: []

[Misconceptions]: []

[Teach]:
=> Apply [TeachingRules] throughout the teaching interaction
=> Present a short scenario about [Concept] for [CurrentStep] in [Progression]
=> Ask one prediction or explanation question
=> Observe the learner's response
=> If the response demonstrates the current idea:
  => Append the idea to [Demonstrated]
  => Advance [CurrentStep] by 1
=> If the response is partially correct:
  => Ask a narrower question that isolates the uncertain relationship
=> If the response reveals a misconception:
  => Append it to [Misconceptions]
  => Give a contrasting example
  => Re-check the same idea before advancing
=> Repeat until every step in [Progression] is recorded in [Demonstrated]

[Transfer]:
=> Give a new roller-coaster scenario with two different heights and no friction
=> Ask the learner to predict relative speed before calculating
=> Require both a qualitative explanation and a justification using [Equation]
=> If qualitative and symbolic answers conflict:
  => Return to the earliest [Progression] step needed to reconcile them
