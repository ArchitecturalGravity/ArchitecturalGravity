# Intent: Compare major household purchases without choosing from sticker price alone by considering:
# - Non-negotiable needs
# - Total ownership cost
# - Practical tradeoffs
# - Uncertainty

Scope:
- Household vehicle purchase comparison

[Need]:
- Replace a primary household vehicle before 2027-03-01
- Expected use: About 10,000 miles per year for at least 6 years
- Typical use is local commuting and errands, with several 500-mile family trips each year

[HardConstraints]:
- Purchase price at or below $42,000 before taxes and registration
- Seats at least 5 adults
- Fits in a garage with 190 inches of usable length
- Can complete the recurring family trip without relying on an uncertain charging stop
- Has independently verifiable crash-test results

[Priorities]:
- Reliability and expected repair burden
- Fuel or energy cost
- Comfort on long trips
- Cargo flexibility
- Insurance cost
- Resale uncertainty

[Candidates]:
- Candidate A: Compact hybrid SUV
- Candidate B: Compact battery-electric SUV
- Candidate C: Midsize gasoline sedan

[Sources]:
- Current manufacturer specifications and warranty terms
- Independent crash-test results
- Local fuel and electricity prices
- Insurance quotes for the household
- Reliability and repair data when available

[Comparison]: []

[Evaluate]:
=> For each item in [Candidates] ↳
  => Verify every [HardConstraints] item from [Sources]
  => Mark unverifiable requirements as unknown rather than satisfied
  => Exclude any candidate that fails a hard constraint
  => Estimate six-year ownership cost using:
    - Purchase price
    - Expected fuel or charging expense
    - Insurance quotes
    - Scheduled maintenance
    - Plausible repair exposure outside warranty
  => Check practical fit against [Need]
  => Record practical tradeoffs against [Priorities]
  => Distinguish:
    - Measured facts
    - Estimates
    - Household preferences
  => Append findings to [Comparison]

[Uncertainty]:
=> Recalculate ownership cost if fuel prices rise 30%
=> Recalculate ownership cost if electricity prices rise 30%
=> Show the effect of a resale value 20% lower than the base estimate
=> Identify any conclusion that changes under these cases

[Choose]:
=> Reject candidates that fail [HardConstraints]
=> Among qualifying candidates, prefer the option whose combined cost and practical fit remain acceptable across [Uncertainty]
=> Do not let a small modeled cost advantage override a material everyday-use disadvantage without stating that tradeoff explicitly
=> If evidence is insufficient to choose confidently, identify the smallest additional fact or test drive needed to resolve the decision

Task:
1. Run [Evaluate]
2. Run [Uncertainty]
3. Apply [Choose]
4. Present:
  - A concise comparison
  - Six-year cost range
  - Hard-constraint results
  - Key uncertainties
  - Recommended purchase
