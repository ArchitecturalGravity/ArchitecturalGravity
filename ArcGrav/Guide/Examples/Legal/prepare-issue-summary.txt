# Intent: Prepare a concise legal issue summary from a supplied case record by
# - Separating:
#   - Allegations
#   - Established facts
#   - Rulings
#   - Unresolved questions
# - Preserving source traceability

[Issue]: Whether the record supports the asserted notice defense and what factual or procedural questions remain unresolved

[Record]:
- ./complaint.pdf
- ./answer.pdf
- ./motion-for-summary-judgment.pdf
- ./opposition.pdf
- ./hearing-transcript.pdf
- ./court-order.pdf

[SourceDiscipline]:
- Attribute party assertions to the party making them
- Treat a fact as established only when:
  - Stipulated
  - Admitted
  - Found by the court
  - Otherwise uncontroverted in the supplied record
- Distinguish record evidence from attorney argument
- Cite the source and any available locator:
  - Page
  - Paragraph
  - Exhibit
  - Transcript line
- Do not supply material absent from [Record], including:
  - Authorities
  - Facts
  - Procedural events

[IssueSummary]:
=> Apply [SourceDiscipline] when interpreting and citing [Record]
=> State [Issue] and its current procedural posture in two or three sentences
=> Organize the material record under:
  - Governing propositions stated or applied in the supplied order or briefing
  - Established or undisputed facts
  - Disputed factual propositions and the evidence cited for each side
  - Prior rulings that narrow or resolve part of the issue
  - Record gaps or ambiguities that prevent resolution
=> For every material proposition, preserve its classification as:
  - Allegation
  - Argument
  - Evidence
  - Admission
  - Court finding
=> Identify contradictions between sources without choosing between them unless the record itself resolves the conflict

Task:
1. Build [IssueSummary] from [Record]
2. Prepare a one-page issue summary suitable for counsel review
3. Append a compact source table linking each material proposition to its record support
4. End with unresolved questions requiring further review across:
  - Facts
  - Procedure
  - Law
