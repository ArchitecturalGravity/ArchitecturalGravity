# Intent: Compare alternative thermal-management concepts for a sealed outdoor electronics enclosure and recommend the design that best satisfies
# - Heat-removal constraints
# - Environmental constraints
# - Maintenance constraints
# - Power constraints

[Need]:
- Reject 180 W of continuous internal heat at 45 C ambient
- Keep the hottest component case at or below 85 C
- Enclosure must remain sealed against dust and wind-driven rain
- Available auxiliary power: 35 W maximum
- Intended unattended service interval: 24 months

[Candidates]:
- `PassiveHeatSink`: External finned heat sink coupled to the internal chassis through a conduction plate
- `HeatPipe`: Internal heat spreader and sealed heat pipes transferring heat to an external fin stack
- `ClosedLoopAir`: Internal and external air circuits separated by an air-to-air heat exchanger

[Evidence]:
- ./thermal-loads.csv
- ./component-limits.md
- ./candidate-test-results.csv
- ./enclosure-envelope.dxf
- ./fan-reliability.md
- ./cost-estimates.csv

[HardConstraints]:
- No candidate may rely on exchanging enclosure air with the environment
- Peak auxiliary electrical load must not exceed 35 W
- Thermal compliance must include measurement uncertainty and the stated 45 C ambient condition
- Any required service interval shorter than 24 months is a constraint failure, not merely a scoring penalty

[TradeSpace]:
- Thermal margin
- Reliability and single-point failures
- Auxiliary power
- Environmental sealing impact
- Manufacturability
- Service burden
- Mass and envelope impact
- Lifecycle cost

[Assessment]: []

[Evaluate]:
=> For each item in [Candidates] ↳
  => Establish the heat-transfer path and the assumptions required for it to work
  => Check [HardConstraints] before applying preference criteria
  => Use [Evidence] to estimate thermal margin against [Need] at the design condition
  => Identify relevant sensitivity to:
    - Fouling
    - Orientation
    - Fan degradation
    - Interface resistance
    - Manufacturing tolerance
  => Record unsupported assumptions and missing evidence separately from demonstrated weaknesses
  => Append the result to [Assessment]

[Select]:
=> Exclude candidates that fail any [HardConstraints]
=> Compare the remaining candidates across [TradeSpace] without collapsing unlike engineering risks into a single unexplained score
=> Prefer the candidate with adequate thermal margin and the fewest consequential lifecycle failure modes
=> If no candidate demonstrates compliance, recommend the smallest next test or design change needed to resolve the decision rather than selecting a nominal winner

Task:
1. Run [Evaluate]
2. Run [Select]
3. Present:
  - Constraint screen
  - Engineering trade matrix
  - Key sensitivities
  - Unresolved evidence gaps
  - Preferred concept if supportable
  - Verification work required before design freeze
