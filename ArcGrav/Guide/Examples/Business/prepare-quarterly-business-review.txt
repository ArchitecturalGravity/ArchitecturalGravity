# Intent: Prepare a quarterly business review that reconciles planned targets with actual performance while explaining material variance from evidence and focusing leadership on forward decisions

[ReviewPeriod]: 2027 Q2

[Audience]:
- Executive leadership
- Business-unit owners

[Sources]:
- ./q2-operating-plan.xlsx
- ./q2-financial-results.xlsx
- ./q2-sales-performance.csv
- ./q2-customer-health.csv
- ./q2-initiative-status.md

[Materiality]:
- Surface a variance when it exceeds 10% of target
- Surface any smaller variance that changes the full-year outlook or requires a leadership decision
- Separate measured results from forecasts and assumptions
- Do not assign a cause unless supported by the source material

[Review]: []

[Synthesize]:
=> Compare actual results in [Sources] with the targets for [ReviewPeriod]
=> Apply [Materiality] when selecting variances and distinguishing evidence from inference
=> For each material variance:
  => Record:
    - Target
    - Actual result
    - Variance
  => Identify evidence-supported drivers
  => Record unresolved explanations as open questions
=> Identify material trends affecting the next quarter across:
  - Customer
  - Pipeline
  - Financial
  - Initiative
=> Distinguish completed outcomes from in-progress work and forecast outcomes
=> Append the resulting findings to [Review]

[QBRSections]:
- Executive summary
- Performance against plan
- Material drivers and exceptions
- Customer and commercial signals
- Strategic initiative status
- Full-year outlook
- Decisions and actions for next quarter

[Prepare]:
=> Populate [QBRSections] from [Review]
=> Prioritize material changes over routine status reporting
=> Tie each conclusion to supporting evidence from [Sources]
=> Make requested decisions explicit, including owner and decision deadline when known
=> Tailor the presentation of [QBRSections] to [Audience]
=> Present the completed quarterly business review

Task:
1. Run [Synthesize]
2. Run [Prepare]
