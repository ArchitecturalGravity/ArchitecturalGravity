[AssessClaims]:

  => Append a claim record to [Claims] with:

    - Source independence
    - Source access and verification from [SourceStatus]
    - Evidence gaps

  - "Verified"
  - "Partially verified"
  - "Unverified"
  - "Disputed"
=> Preserve uncertainty when available evidence does not resolve the claim

[DevelopBrief]:
=> Build [Brief] from [Claims]
=> Include:
  - [ReportingQuestion]

  - Disputed or uncertain claims with explicit status
  - Evidence gaps
  - Reporting directions
  - Publication risks requiring editorial or legal review
=> Exclude confidential source identities from publishable sections
=> Do not present unverified or disputed allegations as established fact

Task:
1. Run [AssessClaims]
