# Intent: Prepare a story from source material while keeping attribution evidence boundaries and unresolved questions visible

[VerificationLog]: ./reporting/verification-log.csv

[SourceMaterial]:
- InterviewTranscript: ./reporting/interview-transcript.txt
- DocumentSet: ./reporting/documents/
- ReportingNotes: ./reporting/notes.md
- VerificationLog: [VerificationLog]

[PublicationRules]:
- On record statements may be attributed by name
- Background material requires publication safe attribution
- Off record material cannot support a publishable claim
- Documents with pending authenticity remain provisional

[StoryClaims]: []

[StoryStructure]: []

[BuildClaims]:
=> For each material claim in [SourceMaterial] ↳
  => Append a claim record to [StoryClaims] with:
    - Exact claim text
    - Supporting interview passage or document location in [SourceMaterial]
    - Attribution mode under [PublicationRules]
    - Verification status from [VerificationLog]
    - Contradicting evidence when present
=> Keep unverified claims in [StoryClaims] with explicit status
=> Do not promote unverified claims into established story facts

[PrepareStory]:
=> Organize verified and attributable material from [StoryClaims] into [StoryStructure] under [PublicationRules]
=> Separate:
  - Established facts
  - Attributed statements
  - Context supported by documents
  - Unresolved questions
=> Preserve source references for every material story element
=> Flag any section that depends on provisional documents
=> Prepare a coherent story plan without filling evidence gaps by inference

Task:
1. Run [BuildClaims]
2. Run [PrepareStory]
3. Provide [StoryStructure] with its source references and unresolved questions
