# Intent: Prepare property acquisition due diligence by separating received evidence required verification unresolved questions and decision-relevant risk

[Transaction]:
- Property: Occupied light industrial facility
- Buyer objective: Confirm acquisition readiness before the diligence deadline
- Decision gate: Proceed to closing only when mandatory diligence is resolved or explicitly accepted by authorized decision makers

[EvidenceRegister]:
- TitleCommitment:
  - Source: ./diligence/title-commitment.pdf
  - Status: Received
  - Authority: Title company
- Survey:
  - Source: ./diligence/alta-survey.pdf
  - Status: Received
  - Authority: Licensed surveyor
- EnvironmentalReport:
  - Source: ./diligence/phase-one-environmental.pdf
  - Status: Received
  - Authority: Environmental consultant
- LeaseAbstracts:
  - Source: ./diligence/lease-abstracts.xlsx
  - Status: Draft
  - Authority: Buyer counsel
- PropertyConditionReport:
  - Source: ./diligence/property-condition-report.pdf
  - Status: Pending
  - Authority: Building consultant

[DiligenceTracks]:
- Title:
  - Confirm exceptions that survive closing
  - Verify access rights serving the parcel
  - Reconcile survey matters with recorded documents
- Environmental:
  - Review recognized environmental conditions
  - Determine whether additional investigation is recommended
  - Preserve consultant limitations and reliance conditions
- Physical condition:
  - Verify major building system condition
  - Identify deferred maintenance with material near-term cost
  - Distinguish observed defects from uninspected conditions
- Occupancy:
  - Reconcile lease obligations with seller representations
  - Identify landlord obligations that transfer at closing
  - Verify material tenant notices or defaults

[RequiredVerification]: []

[OpenQuestions]: []

[RiskRegister]: []

[DecisionReadiness]: []

[ReviewEvidence]:
=> For each track in [DiligenceTracks] ↳
  => Link each conclusion to the controlling item in [EvidenceRegister]
  => Append missing or draft evidence needed for a conclusion to [RequiredVerification]
  => Append contradictions or material ambiguities to [OpenQuestions]
  => Preserve source limitations when they restrict reliance
  => Do not infer a clean condition from the absence of reported evidence

[AssessRisk]:
=> For each unresolved item determine whether it can affect:
  - Legal ownership or access
  - Permitted use
  - Closing obligations
  - Near-term capital cost
  - Continuing occupancy obligations
=> Append decision-relevant items to [RiskRegister] with:
  - Evidence basis
  - Current uncertainty
  - Required resolver
  - Decision consequence
=> Do not mark a risk resolved until the required evidence or authorized acceptance is documented

[GateDecision]:
=> Apply the closing gate in [Transaction]
=> If mandatory diligence remains unresolved append the blocking items to [DecisionReadiness]
=> If a material risk is accepted append the accepting authority and acceptance scope to [DecisionReadiness]
=> If all mandatory diligence is resolved or explicitly accepted append Proceed to closing review to [DecisionReadiness]
=> Preserve nonblocking open questions separately from closing blockers

Task:
1. Run [ReviewEvidence]
2. Run [AssessRisk]
3. Run [GateDecision]
4. Present [RequiredVerification] with [OpenQuestions] [RiskRegister] and [DecisionReadiness]
