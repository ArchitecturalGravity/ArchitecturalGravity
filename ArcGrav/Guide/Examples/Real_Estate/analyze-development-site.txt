# Intent: Analyze a development site through spatial conditions constraints opportunities dependencies and unresolved diligence without converting assumptions into facts

[Project]:
- Program: Mixed-use neighborhood center
- Stage: Pre-acquisition feasibility
- Site: Former commercial parcel at a transit corridor

[SiteGeometry]:
- North edge:
  - Condition: Signalized arterial frontage
  - Relationship: Primary public access candidate
- East edge:
  - Condition: Existing multifamily property
  - Relationship: Sensitive shared boundary
- South edge:
  - Condition: Public alley
  - Relationship: Service access candidate
- West edge:
  - Condition: Transit station entrance
  - Relationship: Highest pedestrian connection

[KnownConditions]:
- Parcel area: 2.6 acres
- Existing improvement: Vacant one-story retail building
- Recorded easement: Utility corridor along the south edge
- Topography: Approximately twelve feet of grade change from north to south
- Stormwater: Existing system capacity not established
- Utility service: Available at the street with project capacity not established

[Constraints]:
- C1: Maintain required access to the recorded utility easement
- C2: Resolve allowable building height under current zoning before fixing massing assumptions
- C3: Protect required fire access through any parking or service concept
- C4: Treat environmental condition as unknown until documented assessment is reviewed
- C5: Do not assume off-site infrastructure capacity from visible service connections

[Opportunities]:
- O1: Concentrate active frontage toward the transit station
- O2: Use the grade change to separate public arrival from service movement
- O3: Place lower building mass near the east boundary if program capacity remains viable
- O4: Reuse existing curb access where transportation review supports it
