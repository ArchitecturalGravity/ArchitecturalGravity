# Intent: Compare architectural design schemes against functional context environmental performance and project constraints without hiding material tradeoffs

Scope:
- Building: Municipal branch library and learning center
- Site: Constrained urban infill parcel
- Stage: Concept design selection

[Schemes]:
- CourtyardScheme:
  - Form: Two-story perimeter bar around a protected courtyard
  - Primary entry: North street frontage
  - Reading rooms: East and south edges
- AtriumScheme:
  - Form: Compact three-story volume around a central atrium
  - Primary entry: Northwest corner
  - Reading rooms: Upper levels around atrium perimeter
- PavilionScheme:
  - Form: Two connected low-rise wings
  - Primary entry: Between wings
  - Reading rooms: Distributed along both wings

[Nonnegotiable]:
- Preserve the required public program area
- Maintain an accessible route to every public level
- Keep construction cost within the approved concept budget
- Avoid permanent encroachment into the recorded utility easement
- Provide a code-compliant means of egress strategy

[Context]:
- North frontage: Highest pedestrian activity
- East boundary: Existing public plaza
- South boundary: Service access and loading
- West boundary: Six-story residential building at close setback
- Climate concern: Summer solar gain and glare
- Community concern: Strong visual connection between interior public space and the plaza

[DecisionCriteria]:
- Functional fit:
  - Adjacency quality
  - Wayfinding clarity
  - After-hours zoning
- Site response:
  - Plaza relationship
  - Service access
  - Neighbor privacy
- Environmental performance:
  - Daylight opportunity
  - Glare exposure
  - Envelope efficiency
- Delivery risk:
  - Structural complexity
  - Construction phasing
  - Cost uncertainty

[Evidence]:
- Program brief: ./program-brief.md
- Concept area schedules: ./scheme-areas.xlsx
- Daylight study: ./daylight-study.pdf
- Preliminary cost model: ./concept-costs.xlsx
- Code review notes: ./code-review.md
- Site constraints plan: ./site-constraints.dwg

[Decision]: []

[Evaluate]:
=> For each item in [Schemes] ↳
  => Check [Nonnegotiable] against [Evidence] before applying preference criteria
  => Trace major program relationships through the proposed organization
  => Evaluate the scheme in [Context] against each group in [DecisionCriteria]
  => Distinguish demonstrated evidence from design assumptions
  => Identify material risks that require further study
  => Append the scheme assessment to [Assessments]

[Compare]:
=> Compare only schemes that remain viable after the nonnegotiable screen
=> Preserve tradeoffs when one scheme performs better in one criterion and worse in another
=> Do not collapse unlike architectural consequences into a single unexplained score
=> Identify the decision-sensitive assumptions that could change the preferred scheme
=> If evidence supports a preference append the preferred scheme and rationale to [Decision]
=> If evidence does not support a preference append the smallest next studies needed to resolve the choice to [Decision]

Task:
1. Run [Evaluate]
2. Run [Compare]
3. Present the constraint screen with [Assessments] and [Decision]
