# Intent: Transform research from several source types into a decision-useful report that distinguishes:
#   - Evidence
#   - Interpretation
#   - Uncertainty

[Research]:
- ./customer-interviews.md
- ./usage-analysis.md
- ./market-review.md
- ./source-notes.md

[ReportBrief]:
- Audience: Product and business leaders deciding whether to expand the pilot
- Decision question: What does the available research support doing next
- Length: 5-7 pages excluding source notes
- Required sections:
  - Executive summary
  - Findings
  - Implications
  - Risks and uncertainties
  - Recommendation
  - Evidence notes
- Tone: Concise and analytical while remaining readable without access to the source material

[EvidenceRules]:
- Attribute material factual claims to the source or sources that support them
- Separate direct observations from interpretations and recommendations
- Preserve meaningful disagreement between sources instead of averaging it away
- State important evidence gaps where they affect confidence in a conclusion
- Do not introduce facts that are not supported by [Research]
- Prefer the strength of evidence over the number of times a point appears

[Transform]:
=> Read [Research] and identify evidence relevant to the decision question in [ReportBrief]
=> Apply [EvidenceRules] throughout report synthesis
=> Group related evidence into findings without preserving the source-by-source order
=> For each major finding, distinguish:
  - What is observed
  - What is inferred
  - How confident the inference is
=> Surface the following near the conclusions they qualify:
  - Contradictions
  - Limitations
  - Missing evidence
=> Derive implications only where the evidence supports them
=> Form a recommendation that answers the decision question and makes its dependencies explicit
=> Write the executive summary after the report body so it reflects the report's actual conclusions
=> Add compact evidence notes that let a reader trace consequential claims back to [Research]

[Review]:
=> Verify every required section in [ReportBrief] is present
=> Verify no recommendation is presented as a research finding
=> Verify uncertainty is not hidden by confident wording
=> Remove detail that does not materially affect the decision or the reader's confidence

Task:
1. Run [Transform]
2. Run [Review]
3. Present the finished report
