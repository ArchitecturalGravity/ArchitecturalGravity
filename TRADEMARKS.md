associated work. We encourage accurate, visible attribution to the original
project while preventing misleading claims of official origin or endorsement.

Copyright permissions are specified in `LICENSING.md`. Apache-2.0 §6 does not

rights. This policy does **not** add a condition to either open license, and does

## Forks and adaptations

Forks may retain legally required Architectural Gravity attribution. The name
of a fork and its presentation should distinguish it from the official project
where necessary to avoid confusion. For example:

> Example Fork — built using materials from the Architectural Gravity project.
> Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Architectural Gravity trademark owner.

That is an illustrative, voluntary format, not a mandatory labeling formula
under Apache-2.0 or CC BY 4.0. A fork must not remove license-required
attribution merely because it uses an independent product name.
